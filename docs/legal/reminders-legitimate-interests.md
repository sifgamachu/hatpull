# Legitimate interests assessment: reminder emails (Hatpull)

_Recorded 2026-10-21. Review before each holiday season._

**Purpose.** Send signed-in members two short reminders about a celebration they joined: one week before and the day before the event date. Content: date, budget, whether they still need to unwrap their name or add wishes, and a link to their celebrations. No advertising, no affiliate links, no Amazon branding.

**Necessity.** Gift exchanges fail when people forget the date or never add a wish list; a reminder is the least intrusive way to prevent that. Only the email address and celebration details already held are used. No profiling.

**Balancing.** Recipients chose to sign in to a celebration they joined and would reasonably expect reminders about it. Low frequency (max two per celebration). Clear notice at sign-in and in the privacy policy. Easy objection: account toggle, one-click unsubscribe link and List-Unsubscribe header in every reminder, honored immediately and recorded on a suppression list.

**Outcome.** Legitimate interests is an appropriate basis. Marketing emails are separate and require opt-in consent (unticked box, consent record: timestamp, text version, source).
